Our Guides

Meet your licensed Gettysburg battlefield guides

Every walking and bus tour is led by a guide certified through the Association of Licensed Battlefield Guides — the same demanding exam used by Gettysburg National Military Park. That means accurate history, no filler, and a guide who can answer the hard questions.

The Team

Historians who live the ground they walk.

Our guides live in and around Gettysburg and Adams County, and each brings a specialty — from artillery tactics on Cemetery Ridge to the civilian story downtown.

TR
Licensed Battlefield Guide, LBG #1142

Tom Reilly

Twenty-two years guiding at Gettysburg. Tom specializes in Day Two, Little Round Top, and the artillery duels along Cemetery Ridge, and he leads many of our Battlefield Highlights and hike departures.

A former park volunteer turned full-time licensed guide, Tom grew up in Adams County and can point out where the fighting reached the fields his family farmed. He's known for making tactics clear to first-time visitors without dumbing down the history.

DC
Licensed Battlefield Guide & former NPS ranger

Denise Coyle

A former National Park Service interpretive ranger, Denise leads our Ghosts of Gettysburg Lantern Walk and civilian-history tours through downtown Gettysburg.

Denise centers the stories of the townspeople, nurses, and families caught in the battle. Her evening lantern walks pair primary-source letters and diaries with the streets and buildings where those events actually happened.

MW
Licensed Battlefield Guide & Historian

Marcus Webb

Author of two regimental histories, Marcus leads our Pickett's Charge programs and the Sunrise Private Battlefield Experience.

Marcus digs into the individual regiments and the human decisions behind the maps. On private tours he tailors the route to your interests, whether that's a specific ancestor's unit, the cavalry fights, or the July 3rd assault on Cemetery Ridge.

JS
Licensed Battlefield Guide

Jenna Serrano

Jenna leads family-friendly walking tours and school groups, with a knack for keeping younger visitors engaged across all three days of the battle.

A certified guide and former classroom teacher from Littlestown, Jenna builds tours that work for mixed-age groups, connecting the landscape to stories kids remember long after they leave the field.
