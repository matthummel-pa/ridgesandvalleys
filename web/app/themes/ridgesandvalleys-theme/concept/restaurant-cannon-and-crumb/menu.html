Good to Know

Menu & dietary questions

Do you label vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free items?

Yes. Use the Vegetarian, Vegan, and Gluten-free filters above to instantly narrow any category. Tags also appear right on each item card.

Where do your ingredients come from?

As close to Gettysburg as we can get them — flour from a mill near Biglerville, produce from Adams County orchards and farm stands, and coffee roasted in small batches just up the road.

Can I order the menu online?

Absolutely. Add items to your order from this page, or head to our Order Online page to check out for pickup on the Square or delivery around downtown.

Do you have allergen information?

Our team is happy to talk through ingredients and cross-contact for any dish — just ask at the counter or send us a note before you visit.
