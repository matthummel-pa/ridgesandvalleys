Catering FAQ

Common questions

How much notice do you need?

Most orders need about 48 hours. For full-event spreads or holiday weekends in Gettysburg, please reach out as early as you can so we can reserve the date.

Do you deliver outside downtown Gettysburg?

Yes — we regularly deliver catering to Biglerville, New Oxford, Littlestown, McSherrystown, Fairfield, Cashtown, and Hanover. Delivery fees vary by distance.

Can you handle dietary restrictions?

Absolutely. We label vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free items, and can build packages around allergies. Just note the details in your request.

Is there a minimum order?

Catering packages generally start around 8–10 guests. For smaller groups, our regular online ordering is often the easier route.
