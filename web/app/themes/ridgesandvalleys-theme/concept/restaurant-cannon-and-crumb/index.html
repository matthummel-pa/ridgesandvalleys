Our Story

Small-batch baking, big local roots.

Cannon & Crumb opened right on Lincoln Square with one idea: bake everything from scratch, source as close to home as possible, and make a cafe this town would actually gather in.

Our flour comes from a mill outside Biglerville, our produce from Adams County orchards and farm stands, and our coffee is roasted in small batches just up the road.
