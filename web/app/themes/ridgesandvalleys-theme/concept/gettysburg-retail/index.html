Our Story

A storefront on York Street, stocked by neighbors

Diamond & Ridge Mercantile opened in an 1899 storefront steps from Lincoln Square with a simple idea: the best souvenir of Adams County isn't a magnet, it's something someone here actually made. Every maker on our shelves is local, and every order is packed by the same people who greet you at the door.
