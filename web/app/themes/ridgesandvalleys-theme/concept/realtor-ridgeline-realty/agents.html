Meet the Team

Your Adams County agents

Three licensed agents, one office on Chambersburg Road, and a shared habit of answering the phone. We live where we sell — and it shows in the way we price, negotiate, and follow through.

Small team, deep roots

Ridgeline Realty is intentionally small. Instead of a rotating cast of part-time agents, you work with the same three people from first showing to closing table — backed by a full-time transaction coordinator who keeps every deadline on track. Between us, we cover the whole Adams County market: the historic district and college neighborhoods in Gettysburg, new construction in Cumberland and Straban Townships, orchard-country homes around Biglerville, and commuter towns like Littlestown, New Oxford, and McSherrystown. When you call our office at (717) 555-0210, you reach a person who has actually walked the streets you're asking about.
