What's Included

Comfort, without the fuss.

Everything a good inn should have — nothing it shouldn't. Every amenity below is included with your stay in downtown Gettysburg.

In Every Stay

Included with your room.

No resort fees and no add-ons — the rate you see is the stay you get.

Full Country Breakfast

Buttermilk biscuits, local sausage, and seasonal fruit served 7:30–10:00am in the dining room or on the porch.

Working Fireplaces

Five of our nine rooms have original wood-burning fireplaces, laid and lit each evening October through March.

Guided Battlefield Walks

Complimentary 90-minute walking tours with a licensed battlefield guide, offered every Saturday morning at 8am.

House-Wide Wi-Fi

Reinforced signal reaching every floor, plus a quiet wired desk in the second-floor library for remote work.

Private Off-Street Parking

A gated lot behind the house — no permits, no meters, no circling downtown Gettysburg for a spot.

Courtyard Garden

A walled garden with a koi pond and Adirondack chairs — open to all guests from dawn until 10pm.

Services

The little things that make a stay.

We're a small house, so we can do the sort of things a big hotel can't — like holding your bags before check-in or pointing you to the quiet spot on the battlefield at sunrise.

Before you arrive

  • Early bag drop and late-afternoon check-in by arrangement.
  • Restaurant reservations on Steinwehr Avenue and around Lincoln Square.
  • Licensed Battlefield Guide bookings for private auto tours.
  • Dietary needs for breakfast — just let us know when you book.

During your stay

  • Complimentary Saturday guided walk from the front porch at 8am.
  • Evening fireplaces laid in cold-weather months.
  • Loaner umbrellas, trail maps, and a shelf of Gettysburg history.
  • Local roast coffee and afternoon lemonade in the parlor.

House Hours

Accessibility

One ground-floor room (the Carlisle Garden Room) is step-free from the rear parking lot. The historic upper floors are reached by stairs only. Call us and we'll talk through what will work for your visit.

Good to Know

Amenity questions.

Is breakfast included for every guest?
Yes — a full country breakfast is included with every room, served 7:30–10:00am. We're glad to accommodate vegetarian, gluten-free, and other dietary needs with a little notice.
How do the guided battlefield walks work?
Our complimentary 90-minute walk leaves the front porch every Saturday at 8am with a licensed battlefield guide. For weekday or private auto tours, we're happy to book a guide for you — see the The Area page for what's nearby.
Do you have parking on site?
We do. Every reservation includes a pass to our gated lot directly behind the inn off Baltimore Street — a genuine convenience in downtown Gettysburg, where street parking is limited.
Can I work remotely from the inn?
Absolutely. Wi-Fi reaches every floor, and there's a quiet wired desk in the second-floor library if you need a reliable connection for calls.
