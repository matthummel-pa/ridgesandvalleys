Good to Know

Room questions, answered.

Which rooms have working fireplaces?
Five of our nine rooms — including the Chamberlain and Seminary rooms — have original wood-burning fireplaces, laid and lit each evening from October through March.
Is parking really included?
Yes. Every reservation includes a pass to our gated lot behind the house on Baltimore Street — no meters, no permits, and no circling downtown Gettysburg for a spot.
How far is the inn from the battlefield?
The Baltimore Street entrance to Gettysburg National Military Park is about a 0.4-mile walk. See the The Area page for walking distances and driving directions.
Do you serve breakfast every day?
We do — a full country breakfast is served 7:30–10:00am daily in the dining room or on the porch, and it's included with every room. Details are on the Amenities page.
