Rates & Policies

Simple, direct booking

What goes into the number you see above.

Room rates

Heritage Double $189, Ridge King $219, Field Suite $259, Cupola Loft $299 per night. A flat $15/night amenity fee covers breakfast, Wi-Fi, and battlefield bike rentals.

Guests & taxes

Rates include two guests; each additional guest is $18/night. Pennsylvania and local occupancy taxes total about 9% and are shown in the taxes & fees line.

Check-in / out

Check-in from 4:00pm, check-out by 11:00am. Early check-in and late check-out are free when available — request them from the amenities page or at the desk.

Cancellation

Cancel up to 48 hours before check-in for a full refund. Within 48 hours, one night's rate applies. Documented emergencies are always fully refundable.
