07 — Do & Don't

Keeping it on-brand.

Do

  • Lead with the landscape — ridgelines, valleys, farmland, warm light.
  • Pair deep pine with one bold clay or wheat accent.
  • Use one italic serif word to carry each headline.
  • Keep generous white (cream) space; let it breathe.
  • Grade photos warm and calm; soft, natural light.

Don't

  • No cannons, battlefield silhouettes, flags, or parchment.
  • No Old-West or Civil-War display fonts; no heavy red-white-blue.
  • No neon "digital agency" gradients or cold tech blues/purples.
  • Don't set the serif in long paragraphs — accents only.
  • Don't crowd the layout; avoid busy, stocky corporate imagery.
