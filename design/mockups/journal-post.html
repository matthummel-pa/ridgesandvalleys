The 5 things every Gettysburg business should fix on Google first

July 14, 2026 · 6 min read · Matt Hummel

Before you spend a dollar on a new website, there's a shorter list worth handling first — and most of it is free. When a visitor searches for your shop, Google decides what they see before your site ever loads.

1. Claim and complete your Google Business Profile

It's the single highest-leverage thing a local business can do. Hours, photos, category, and a link — filled in and kept current — put you on the map, literally.

2. Make your hours impossible to get wrong

Inconsistent hours between Google, Facebook, and your site quietly cost you visits. Pick one source of truth and mirror it everywhere.

"A visitor who can't find your hours in five seconds doesn't call — they scroll to the next result."

3. Add the basics customers actually search

  • Directions and parking
  • What you sell, in plain words
  • A phone number that taps to call on mobile

None of this requires a redesign. It requires someone to sweat the details — which is exactly the work we do before we ever touch a template.
